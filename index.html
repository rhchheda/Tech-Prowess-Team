01 — Vision

What is Tech Prowess?

A structured cross-disciplinary innovation program that transforms campus talent into a direct incubation pipeline — from problem statement to Cshine.

🎯
The Problem
10,000 students across diverse disciplines — yet innovation happens in silos. CS students build without domain context. Domain students lack technical depth. Potential is trapped.
💡
The Solution
A formal program where cross-disciplinary teams are mandated — not optional. Every team must represent at least 2 branches. Ideas go through structured review with actionable feedback.
🚀
The Outcome
Shortlisted ideas feed directly into Cshine's incubation pipeline. Best teams get mentors, certificates, badges, and a public demo day — creating real career impact.
📐
The Scale
Built for 10K students from day one. Email OTP tied to @kletech.ac.in ensures zero friction, verified identity, and zero credential storage overhead.

03 — Problem Statements

Structured Problem Design

Problem statements aren't just titles. Each PS has a template, a domain tag, a difficulty tier, a target audience, and a defined deadline — ensuring quality and fairness.

PS Template Fields
What Every PS Must Contain
Title, domain tag, difficulty tier, context background, expected deliverable type (prototype / research / business model), eligibility (year, branch), deadline, and PS owner contact.
Visibility Rules
Who Sees What
PS can be set as Open (all students), Targeted (specific branches or years), or Private (invite-only for industry partner challenges). Admin controls visibility per PS.
Deadline Management
What Happens at Deadline
Submissions lock automatically. Partial drafts are auto-saved but flagged as incomplete. Admin receives a summary of all submissions received before closure.
Who Posts PS
PS Ownership
Faculty, Cshine coordinators, or approved industry partners. Each PS has a named owner who is notified on every submission and is responsible for reviewer assignment.

04 — Reviewer Logic

Fair, Structured Review

Reviewer assignment isn't manual guesswork. A defined logic handles assignment, conflict checks, escalation, and panel sizing — automatically.

ASSIGNMENT
How Reviewers Are Assigned
Auto-assigned by domain tag match from Reviewers sheet. Round-robin within same domain to balance load. Admin can override manually if needed.
CONFLICT CHECK
Conflict of Interest Rule
Reviewer's department is cross-checked against each team member's branch. If a match is found, that reviewer is skipped and next eligible one is assigned.
PANEL SIZE
How Many Reviewers per Submission
Standard: 1 reviewer. Escalated / borderline submissions: panel of 3. Panel average score used for final decision. Majority rules on Approve / Reject.
MANDATORY REMARKS
No Decision Without Feedback
Submission field is locked until remarks are filled. Minimum 50-character feedback required before any decision is saved. Prevents empty rejections.
Assigned→ 48hr No Response→ Reminder Email→ 24hr Still Pending→ Admin Alert→ Reassigned

06 — Eligibility Rules

Who Can Participate?

Clear eligibility rules prevent ambiguity, ensure fairness across year groups, and protect the program's academic standing.

🎓
Year Requirement
All years eligible. Beginner-tier PS reserved for 1st & 2nd year. Advanced PS open to 3rd year and above only.
⚠️
Backlog Standing
Students with active backlogs may participate but are flagged. Admin can set a policy per cohort — allowed / restricted / limited slots.
🔒
Active Team Limit
A student may be in at most 1 active team at a time. Cross-team membership detected and blocked at invite acceptance.
🏫
Discipline Verification
Branch auto-populated from Students sheet on login. Students cannot self-declare branch — sourced from university records only.

08 — Drafts & Versions

Nothing is Lost

Auto-saved drafts and full version history on every resubmission — reviewers see exactly what changed, students never lose work.

💾
DRAFT AUTOSAVE
Idea saved every 60 seconds
Students can close the browser and return — their draft is exactly where they left it, stored in the Drafts sheet against their Team-ID and PS-ID.
📤
VERSION 1 — ORIGINAL SUBMISSION
First submission locks as v1
On final submit, draft is promoted to Submissions sheet as Version 1. Draft row is cleared. A confirmation email card is dispatched to all team members.
🔄
VERSION 2 — AFTER REVISION
Resubmission stored as v2 alongside v1
On resubmission, the updated content is saved as Version 2. Both versions visible to the reviewer side-by-side. Reviewer must acknowledge what changed before proceeding.
🗃️
ARCHIVE
All versions retained permanently
Even after final decision, all versions are retained in the Idea_Versions sheet. Admin can retrieve full history for any submission at any time for audits or disputes.

11 — Cohort Management

Season by Season

Each program run is a cohort — semester-bounded, independently tracked, and comparable across seasons for institutional analytics.

📅
Cohort-based Structure
Each semester = one cohort. All PS, teams, and submissions are tagged with a cohort_id for clean separation and cross-cohort reporting.
🔒
Cohort Closure
At semester end, admin closes the cohort. All open submissions are force-archived. A cohort summary report is auto-generated and emailed to admin.
📊
Cross-cohort Analytics
Admin dashboard shows cohort-over-cohort trends: submissions, approval rate, branch participation, reviewer turnaround time.
🔁
Rolling vs Semester
Default: semester-based. Admin can switch to rolling mode per cohort — useful for industry partner challenges with custom timelines.

13 — Program Feedback

Continuous Improvement

Anonymous feedback after every cohort — on the program, the PS quality, and reviewer fairness. Feeds the admin dashboard so every season is better than the last.

🙈
Fully Anonymous
No USN, no email, no team ID linked to feedback. Students are more honest when they know there's no trace. Triggered by an email at cohort closure.
⭐
Structured Rating
5-point rating on: PS quality, portal usability, reviewer fairness, and overall experience. Plus a free-text comment field for detailed input.
📈
Admin Dashboard
Aggregated per cohort — average scores, comment word cloud, and trend lines across seasons. Exportable to PDF for authority review presentations.
🔁
Action Loop
Each cohort's feedback summary is reviewed by the program committee before the next cohort opens. Changes made are acknowledged in the next PS announcement email.
