01 — Vision

What is Tech Prowess?

A structured cross-disciplinary innovation program that transforms campus talent into a direct incubation pipeline — from problem statement to Cshine.

🎯
The Problem
10,000 students across diverse disciplines — yet innovation happens in silos. CS students build without domain context. Domain students lack technical depth. Potential is trapped.
💡
The Solution
A formal program where cross-disciplinary teams are mandated — not optional. Every team must represent at least 2 branches. Ideas go through structured review with actionable feedback.
🚀
The Outcome
Shortlisted ideas feed directly into Cshine's incubation pipeline. Best teams get mentors, certificates, badges, and a public demo day — creating real career impact.
📐
The Scale
Built for 10K students from day one — no passwords, no credential management. Email OTP authentication tied to @kletech.ac.in ensures zero friction and verified identity.

RULE 01
Cross-discipline Mandatory
Every team must have members from at least 2 different branches. Single-discipline submissions are blocked at the point of team formation.
RULE 02
Team Lead Accountability
One designated Lead submits the idea, owns all communication, and is the point of contact for reviewer remarks. All co-members must accept via email CTA.
RULE 03
Max 2 Resubmissions
Rejected ideas may be revised and resubmitted up to twice. After the second rejection, the submission is archived — preventing endless revision loops.
RULE 04
Dropout Protocol
If a member drops, the team has 48 hours to replace or dissolve. Admin is auto-notified. Prevents ghost teams from occupying active problem statement slots.

05 — Review & Scoring

Weighted, Blind Review

Four criteria, transparent weightage, optional blind scoring to eliminate bias. Per-reviewer approval rate tracked by admin for governance.

Innovation & Originality30%
Feasibility & Execution25%
Cross-discipline Utilisation25%
Clarity of Presentation20%
Review Safeguards
Blind review option — reviewer sees submission without team or student names
Reviewer cannot be the submitting student's own faculty guide
Per-reviewer approval rate visible to admin for bias detection
All remarks are mandatory — no decision without written feedback
Shortlisted ideas automatically flagged for Cshine incubation pipeline
